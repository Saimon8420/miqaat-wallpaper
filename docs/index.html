What it does

Quiet on the surface.
Exact underneath.

Reminders

The right words at the right time

  • Dua, dhikr, istighfar, durood, a Quran verse or a sahih hadith, one at a time, on the schedule you choose
  • Morning adhkar from Fajr to sunrise, evening adhkar after Asr, adhkar after each salah for 15 minutes
  • Durood and the Surah al-Kahf reminder on Fridays; duas before sleep late at night
  • Arabic, meaning in English or Bangla, and the exact reference on every one
Memory
~30 MB

Measured on a two-monitor 1080p desktop. Redraws about once a minute (every 2 seconds for the small system strip, if on) and pauses during games and full-screen video.

Special days

Only what is authentic

  • White Days, Mondays and Thursdays, Ashura, the Day of Arafah
  • The first ten days of Dhul Hijjah, the last ten nights of Ramadan, Eid, six days of Shawwal
  • What to do, with its hadith, and a heads-up the day before
System monitor

Your PC at a glance, optional

  • CPU, memory, GPU, disk and network in one quiet row with small history lines
  • Read straight from Windows; only that small strip is redrawn
  • Turn it off and the wallpaper goes back to updating once a minute
Authenticity. No text was typed from memory: every word is pulled from published datasets by a build script, and only the references were chosen by hand. Hadith come only from collections with their grading shown; adhkar from Hisn al-Muslim with item numbers. Days whose special worship is disputed are not presented as worship days.
Under the hood

Where the words come from,
and when things refresh.

Source

A library that ships inside the app

  • Quran: the holy-quran library (MIT): Hafs text with English (Sahih International) and Bangla translations; 237 short, complete verses chosen for a wallpaper
  • Hadith: the free hadith-api (public domain) in Arabic, English and Bangla: 224 hadith from Bukhari, Muslim, Tirmidhi, Abu Dawud, Ibn Majah and an-Nawawi's Forty, each with its grading
  • Duas, dhikr, istighfar and durood: 220 items from Hisn al-Muslim (Fortress of the Muslim), with the book's item number on each
  • All of it is one 1 MB file built into the app, so reminders never need the internet
Choosing

How each reminder is picked

  • Each kind (dua, dhikr, verse, hadith…) has its own shuffled deck, so nothing repeats until the whole deck has been shown
  • The time of day comes first: morning adhkar after Fajr, adhkar for 15 minutes after each salah, evening adhkar after Asr, duas before sleep at night
  • On special days, that day's sunnah is shown first (Friday: al-Kahf and durood; White Days and Monday/Thursday: fasting)
  • Quran verses are picked from a curated set of short, complete verses, never cut mid-meaning
Refreshing

Almost everything happens locally

  • Clock and countdown: once a minute (once a second only if you turn seconds on)
  • Reminder: every 5, 10, 15, 30 or 60 minutes, your choice, with a slow fade
  • Prayer times: a whole month is fetched from the Miqaat API once and saved; it's fetched again only at the start of a new month or when you change city, method or Asr time
  • Light and scene: shifts at each prayer time
  • System monitor: every 2 seconds if it's on
  • Content updates: the library is part of the app; new or corrected texts arrive with app updates
Offline
1 request a month

If the internet is down, the saved month keeps working, and the reminders never needed the internet at all.

Install

One download.
No admin, no account.

01

Download MiqaatWallpaper-Setup.exe (about 30 MB) from the latest release.

02

Run it. Windows may show "Windows protected your PC" because the app is new and not code-signed: click More info, then Run anyway.

03

Choose Yes to install for your account only. It copies itself to your user folder, adds a Start menu entry and starts with Windows. Choose No to just try it once.

04

Set your city. Settings open on first run; later, right-click the moon icon near the clock. Changes show on the wallpaper straight away.

05

Uninstall any time from Settings › Apps › Installed apps › Miqaat Wallpaper. Your normal wallpaper comes back.

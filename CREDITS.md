## Words and data

- **Prayer times:** the [Miqaat API](https://miqaat-dev.vercel.app) (calculations by the open-source *adhan* library).
- **Quran:** the [holy-quran](https://www.npmjs.com/package/holy-quran) library (MIT): Hafs text, English translation by Sahih International, and a Bangla translation. Surah names from [quran-meta](https://www.npmjs.com/package/quran-meta).
- **Hadith:** [hadith-api](https://github.com/fawazahmed0/hadith-api) by Fawaz Ahmed (Unlicense, public domain), with the gradings it carries.
- **Adhkar, duas, istighfar and durood:** *Hisn al-Muslim* (Fortress of the Muslim) by Sa'id ibn Wahf al-Qahtani, via [hisnmuslim.com](https://www.hisnmuslim.com).
- **City search:** [Open-Meteo geocoding](https://open-meteo.com/en/docs/geocoding-api) (CC BY 4.0).
